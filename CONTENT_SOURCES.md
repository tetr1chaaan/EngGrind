## Rules before adding content

1. Prefer content created by EngGrind or commissioned with a written agreement that covers the intended use, edits, distribution, and commercial use.
2. For third-party material, record the title, creator/rightsholder, original source URL, exact license/version, date checked, and any edits here. Keep a copy or archived record of the license and source.
3. Verify the license applies to the actual asset and permits the planned use. A Creative Commons label is not a blanket clearance for third-party rights, and restrictions vary by license. Avoid NC material in a paid product and ND material if the content will be adapted.
4. For exam-provider material, audio, transcripts, questions, logos, or branded images, get written permission for the exact commercial use before publishing. Do not scrape or lightly rewrite official questions as a substitute for permission.
5. Use “IELTS-style practice” only to describe compatibility, without logos or visual imitation, and show a clear independent-product disclaimer. Never imply an official score or endorsement.
6. Preserve attribution and license notices in the UI or a directly linked credits page whenever a license requires it. Make edits clear where required.

This is a product-content workflow, not a legal opinion. Confirm rights for the target launch countries and commercial model with qualified counsel when moving from prototype to release.
